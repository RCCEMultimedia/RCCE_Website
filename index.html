A Church for Everyone

Rooted in Love.
Built on Faith.

At Revival Christian Church of Enfield, everyone is welcome — regardless of your background, nationality, age or where you are on your spiritual journey. We are a diverse, multicultural community rooted in the love of Christ, and we take that seriously.

Since 2009 we have been serving the Enfield community through weekly worship, prayer, Bible study, children and youth programmes, and hands-on social action. We would love for you to be part of what God is doing here.

Life at Revival

Everything You Need
to Grow and Thrive

01

Worship

Every Sunday we gather to encounter the living God — through passionate worship, Spirit-led prayer, and teaching rooted deeply in Scripture. Whether you join us in person or online, you are part of something real.

Grow

Faith that stays still, stagnates. We run weekly home Bible study groups, discipleship programmes, and youth ministry — so that every member of your family has space to grow in their relationship with God.

Dr Nick Chanda and Dr Mulenga Chanda lead Revival with a rare combination of visionary leadership and pastoral warmth. Together since the church's founding in May 2009, they have built a congregation where people from every background feel genuinely at home.

They lead not from a platform, but from a life of prayer, service and deep love for God and people.

Social Action

Faith That Gets
Into the Street

At Revival Christian Church of Enfield, we believe that genuine faith always finds its way into the street. The words of Jesus in Matthew 25 are not a metaphor — they are a mandate. When we feed the hungry, clothe the vulnerable, and stand with the lonely and marginalised, we are doing it for Him.

"For I was hungry, and you fed me. I was thirsty, and you gave me a drink. I was a stranger, and you invited me into your home."

— Matthew 25:35 (NLT)
