Our Pastors

Meet Dr Nick Chanda and Dr Mulenga Chanda — founders and Senior Pastors of Revival Church Enfield since 2009.

Senior Pastors

Called to Lead, Serve and Love

Dr Nick Chanda and Dr Mulenga Chanda are our Senior Pastors. They both served in Jesus House London for a combined 21 years and were commissioned to set up Revival Christian Church of Enfield in 2009.

Senior Pastor

Dr Nick Chanda

Dr Nick Chanda is the Founding Senior Pastor. Simply passionate for Revival, he is a great lover of the Bible and prayer. A man of deep conviction and quiet strength, Dr Nick leads with a servant's heart and a fierce love for God and people.

"A great lover of the Bible and prayer — and anything chicken!"

His vision, first ignited during 21 combined years at Jesus House London, continues to bear fruit as Revival grows in its calling to transform Enfield and beyond.

Senior Pastor

Dr Mulenga Chanda

Dr Mulenga Chanda is our Senior Pastor with a passion for excellence, prayer and people. Her heart beats especially for women stepping into their God-given calling — equipping and empowering them to walk in purpose and destiny.

"Her interests include shopping, reading, cooking, baking and helping people. A lover of vegetables to the extent they do count as a meal in her world!"

Dr Mulenga brings warmth, wisdom and unwavering faith to everything she does — in the pulpit, in the community, and at home.
