How It All Began

Revival Christian Church of Enfield was commissioned on 17th May 2009 by Dr Nick Chanda and Dr Mulenga Chanda, following 21 combined years of service at Jesus House London. They were sent to plant a church in Enfield that would bring revival, refreshing and transformation to the community.

From that inaugural Sunday in May 2009, Revival has grown into a vibrant, multicultural family of faith — welcoming people of all races, backgrounds and ages to experience the life-changing love of Jesus Christ.

Our Purpose

What We Are Here For

Our primary aim is to see transformation of individual lives through the wider community, to experience refreshing times and have fun in God's presence.

Our Community

Built on People

Dr Nick and Dr Mulenga lead closely with a dedicated team of trustees and volunteers. Together they oversee weekly services, prayer gatherings and activities for men, women, teenagers and children.

Revival Christian Church of Enfield welcomes people of all races, backgrounds and ages. Whether you are searching for faith, new to the area, or simply looking for a community that cares — you are welcome here.
