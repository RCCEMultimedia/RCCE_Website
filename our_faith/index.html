Our Faith

Rooted in the love of Jesus Christ — love remains central in all that we do.

Jesus paid the price in love for us and we believe in loving one another and loving the wider community — not just in words but in deeds. Love remains central in all that we do.

Statement of Faith

What We Believe

We believe in the death and resurrection of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. Romans 4:25 says He was delivered over to death for our trespasses and was raised to life for our justification.

This truth shapes everything we do — how we worship, how we serve, how we love one another and the wider community of Enfield.

Core Beliefs

The Foundations of Our Faith

Resurrection

We believe in the bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead.

Salvation

We believe salvation is through faith in Jesus Christ alone.

Baptism

We believe in baptism in the name of the Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the power and gifts of the Holy Spirit.
