Revival Christian Church of Enfield

Our Vision

Since 2009, driven by a single vision — revival in Enfield and beyond.

Our Purpose

One Vision, Unwavering

Since our inaugural service on 17th May 2009, we have been driven by a single vision: to see revival in Enfield and beyond.

Five Pillars

How We Will See Revival

01
Prayer

Souls Saved & Transformed

Through progress and seeing spiritual transformation in individual lives and the wider community.

02
Strategic Partnership

A Church of Influence

Through strategic partnership and alliances to influence policies and shape the culture around us.

03
Empowerment

Vulnerable People Supported

Through community engagement that will support vulnerable people and reduce isolation.

04
Personal Development

Training & Entrepreneurship

Through education and development of people to reduce illiteracy and encourage entrepreneurship.

05
Destiny

The Governing Church

We aim to raise a body of believers who understand God's purpose for their lives and walk in their God-given destinies by exhibiting order, structure and excellence in all endeavours.
