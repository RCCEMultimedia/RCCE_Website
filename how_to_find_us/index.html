Getting Here

By Bus, Tube or Car

By Bus

Several bus routes serve London Road, Enfield. Check Transport for London (TfL) for live route and timing information to the EN2 area.

By Tube

The nearest London Underground station is Enfield Town (Greater Anglia / Overground). From there, take a short bus or taxi ride along London Road to reach The Revive Centre.

By Car

The Revive Centre is located at 47 London Road, Enfield EN2 6DS. Street parking is available nearby on surrounding roads. Please check local signage for restrictions.

What to Wear

You Are Free to Be You!!!
