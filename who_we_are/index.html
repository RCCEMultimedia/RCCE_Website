Our Identity

A Church for Everyone

We are a diverse, multi-generational, multicultural church rooted in the love of Christ, founded in 2009. Everyone is welcome — regardless of background, age, or nationality.

We believe the Church is at its best when it reflects the full beauty of God's family — people from every walk of life, gathered in worship, prayer and community.

Children's Ministry

We Value Your Kids

We have experienced, DBS-checked teachers who are passionate about equipping children in their faith. We value your kids and believe they are already an inspirational influence.

Our children's programme runs every Sunday during the service, giving your children a safe, fun and faith-filled environment of their own.

Youth Ministry

Youth Revived

Youth Revived is our youth ministry. We equip young people to have a personal relationship with God — vibrant, spirit-filled youth who read the Bible, pray, and find great fun along the way.
