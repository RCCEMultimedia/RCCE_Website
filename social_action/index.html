Faith in Action

At Revival Christian Church of Enfield, we believe genuine faith always finds its way into the street. The words of Jesus in Matthew 25 are not a metaphor — they are a mandate.

"For I was hungry and you gave me food, I was a stranger and you welcomed me."
Matthew 25:35

Our Programmes

How We Serve

Food Support

We run a food pantry providing essential groceries to families in need in Enfield. No one should go hungry.

Pastoral Care

Our pastoral team offers emotional and spiritual support to individuals and families facing difficult times.

Community Events

Throughout the year we run community events open to all — bringing people together to celebrate, connect and give back.

Our Foundation

Matthew 25 Foundation

Our social action work is inspired by and operated through the Matthew 25 Foundation — putting faith into action for Enfield's most vulnerable.
